**Why it is misleading:** including moisture and processing method makes the model look carefully adjusted, which lends the altitude coefficient unearned credibility. What changes is its size and its meaning: the corrected coefficient is a within-country comparison, and more than half of the Part 1 effect was country differences wearing altitude's clothing.
